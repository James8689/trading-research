# Scientific state machine

These are design contracts, not implemented transitions. Existing B3 experiment rules remain authoritative for those IDs. This document cannot silently alter a frozen threshold or date.

## Candidate states and required evidence

**Proposed:** all eight mechanism questions answered, stable ID, known prior art and baseline overlap reviewed. Register every proposed family, including discarded variants. Similar names or minor thresholds do not create a new independent hypothesis.

**Feasibility pending:** predeclared source sample and missing-field denominator. No price-outcome selection. Mechanism investigator and data auditor inspect independently; critical timestamps, mandatory participant behavior, instrument support and costs must be verifiable. Outcomes are feasible, data-blocked, rejected, or insufficient evidence.

**Plan frozen:** director signs exact universe, signal/knowledge clock, deterministic entries/exits/sizing, cash/settlement, costs, latency, corporate actions, estimand, controls, development/untouched periods, statistics, concentration and fail criteria. Independent critique resolved or recorded as a blocking objection. Hash plan, prompts for extraction, source protocol and code specification before acquiring new outcomes.

**Development:** reviewed Python on immutable event/data snapshots. Include failures, missing cases and delisted outcomes; reconcile cash/shares and demonstrate timing with tests. Record every executed variant in a trial ledger. Unexpected results may suggest a new version; they may not revise the current freeze.

**Validation reserved:** suitable development evidence, full code review and prospective window not yet consumed. Custodian has separate outcome access. Workers tuning rules receive completeness/latency metadata only. A shared directory plus a prompt is not a real information barrier; until access separation is implemented, label the holdout protected by procedure, not technically sealed.

**Validation evaluated:** release at the frozen time, once, under unchanged rules. Too few events or missing critical coverage = inconclusive. Failures stay failures. A favorable subset, year or cost assumption cannot override a failed primary metric.

**Execution observation:** predeclared 30–60 sessions as required by the active plan (B3: 60 plus its minimum trade count), live quotes and locally recorded hypothetical orders only. Capture missed decisions/fills, latency, outages and adverse selection. Never equate displayed quotes or broker paper fills with guaranteed execution.

**Implementation consideration:** complete evidence packet with costs, out-of-sample, concentration, risk and observation gates. Human review remains; no automatic live-trading transition exists. User's no-live-trading instruction persists.

At every state, `rejected`, `blocked`, `inconclusive` and `paused_scope` are distinct. Rejected means a stated premise/test failed; blocked means an external input is missing; inconclusive means available evidence is insufficient; paused_scope means current authorization does not include the next operation.

## Rejection and reopening

Store hypothesis text, version, economic family, tested rules, source/result hashes, decision reason, conflicting evidence and reopening condition. Do not repeatedly rediscover old failures. Reopen only with a new observable mechanism, genuinely new data that resolves the blocker, or a corrected documented implementation error. The old version is preserved. A corrected bug requires rerunning declared tests and acknowledging validation contamination.

## Multiple testing across the whole project

An endless search creates false positives even when each batch tests only two candidates. Maintain a global ledger of generated families, explored variants, tested statistics and every viewed outcome window. Batch 3's local multiplicity correction does not cover future batches. Before later confirmatory campaigns, freeze campaign-wide error control or designate all search results exploratory and reserve independent future confirmation. Never reuse a future confirmation window after inspecting it.

## Advancement is an artifact decision

A worker's JSON decision is advisory. A gate record identifies the candidate/version, prior/next state, required artifacts and hashes, reviewer, unresolved objections, numerical checks and authorization boundary. Controller validates existence/integrity and allowed transition; frontier reviewer assesses substance. No amount of confident prose replaces missing measurements.

## When the loop stops

Stop on exhausted budget, unknown usage, no authorized ready tasks, source access failure, information-barrier breach, repeated malformed output, or candidate escalation requiring review. Reject weak ideas promptly. Continue promising ones only with a concrete next falsifier. If nothing survives, save "no candidate" and stop; do not relax standards to produce a winner. Future validation may require months of elapsed market time, which extra agents cannot accelerate.
