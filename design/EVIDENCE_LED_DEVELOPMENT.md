# Evidence-led development and blueprint readiness

Design requirements adopted September 9, 2026. These strengthen the existing roadmap without changing frozen experiments or claiming new runtime enforcement.

## 1. Research cycles must justify architectural growth

The next delivery is a minimal opportunity/family registry plus one real, bounded document-feasibility cycle through the existing manual network. Start with B3-H1-v1 under its frozen sample protocol. Register the mechanism, sources, missing fields, independent findings, director decision and retrospective. Rejecting or blocking the candidate with evidence completes this learning cycle; there is no requirement to manufacture a survivor or blueprint.

Do not build semantic infrastructure, many provider adapters, a large worker population or a continuing scheduler as prerequisites to this cycle. Initially, explicit family IDs, ordinary text search and reviewed relationship links suffice. Keep domain-neutral fields while exercising them on the existing candidate. Do not replace independent checks with one worker simply to simplify the trial.

After the cycle, the director records the largest observed bottleneck, examples and an estimated cost of leaving it unresolved. A proposed architectural addition must identify:

- The observed failure or repeated work it addresses, with task IDs.
- The smallest change that could resolve it and a simpler alternative.
- A predeclared benefit metric, regression checks and bounded implementation cost.
- An acceptance decision and rollback/removal condition.

Add roles or providers only when evidence supports their marginal benefit. Known correctness defects can be repaired immediately; they do not require repeated failures. Unverified model diversity and a growing number of components are not progress measures. No paid allowance is inferred from this sequence; the manual cycle remains possible before a provider adapter.

## 2. Improvement is measured, not inferred from changes

Keep memory/context, prompt and routing versions separate. Before automatic adaptation, collect an error taxonomy and baseline across real tasks. Record both successes and failures, including correct rejection, blocking and inconclusive decisions. An isolated bad response can trigger investigation or containment, but not an automatic prompt rewrite.

A recurring-pattern proposal needs at least two independently reviewed instances on distinct task/source cases, plus the total eligible-case denominator and counterexamples. Two is a minimum admission check, not statistical evidence of improvement. Repeated retries of the same case do not qualify. If cases are insufficient, retain a proposed investigation without modifying active behavior.

Freeze a comparison before inspecting evaluation outcomes. Declare the component changed, baseline/candidate versions, representative held-out cases, blinded reviewer rubric, minimum sample size, primary metric, allowed cost and noninferiority margins for secondary metrics. Fix thresholds before results. Evaluate:

- Factual and citation accuracy, including whether quotations support the claim.
- Coverage of required fields and correct handling of unknowns.
- Critical scientific errors and false advancement/rejection.
- Repeated investigations already resolved by durable memory.
- Actual model/data cost, reviewer effort and elapsed time per correctly completed task.

Synthetic fixtures verify code paths only. Exact disposition labels alone cannot certify reasoning quality. Unknown monetary usage remains unknown; arbitrary cost units cannot establish financial savings. Account for every attempted comparison and consumed evaluation set. Insufficient or ambiguous results mean inconclusive, with the baseline retained.

Adoption requires the predeclared primary improvement, no observed critical errors on the evaluated suite, and all secondary regression/cost limits. Zero observed errors is not a guarantee of zero future errors. An independent reviewer signs the evidence packet. Change one component at a time unless a factorial or similarly explicit comparison can distinguish contributions.

After adoption, use a bounded probation batch with predefined audit sampling and rollback triggers. Preserve the previous version. A critical regression pauses affected advancement and rolls back the component where appropriate; it does not erase the failed adoption. Lessons and retrieval changes receive the same provenance and regression discipline as prompts. Routing changes must preserve scientific role quality, not merely reduce the invoice.

Current implementation gap: improvement.py enforces an initial synthetic comparison gate, but not this recurring-pattern admission, substantive reasoning rubric, measured monetary cost or probation protocol. Do not enable automatic adoption until these contracts are implemented and tested. Do not describe the current scripted demo as a measured improvement.

## 3. Builder review precedes implementation-ready status

Use distinct states: research_validated -> blueprint_draft -> builder_feasibility_review -> clarification_or_revalidation -> implementation_ready. A builder review can instead yield blocked, infeasible or inconclusive. Research validation and build feasibility are separate requirements; neither can substitute for the other.

At early research stages, record obvious execution dependencies and check the cheapest potentially fatal ones before expensive testing. Later, an independent builder reviewer inspects the exact versioned draft before the terminal ready label. This reviewer assesses feasibility; it does not deploy or operate the opportunity.

The review must establish or explicitly block on:

- Actual data/API access and allowed use, including required historical and live fields.
- Necessary account features, venue or platform capabilities and automation limits.
- Attainable timing, latency, fill/delivery capacity and reconciliation assumptions.
- Complete operating costs, capital/resource needs and realistic unit economics.
- Deterministic behavior, failure recovery, monitoring and acceptance-test reproducibility.

Use domain-appropriate evidence and bounded read-only or local probes where authorized. A document's assertion that an API exists is not verified access. Unknown critical dependencies block readiness. No trial purchase, public customer contact, funded order or production deployment is authorized by the review requirement alone.

Every clarification has an ID, blueprint/version/hash, questioned assumption, evidence, alternatives, impact, owner and status. Research responds with a source-backed clarification or a new blueprint version. If a response changes data timing, behavior, costs, scope or another material validated assumption, return to the affected research gate and rerun the required tests. A wording-only correction records why revalidation is unnecessary. Preserve dissent and unresolved questions.

The ready gate requires complete research evidence, builder review of the current hash, no unresolved critical questions, reproducible acceptance artifacts and explicit sign-off by research and independent builder reviewer. Any material revision invalidates prior sign-off until reviewed again. Implementation-ready means sufficiently specified and feasible to build within the tested scope, not guaranteed profit or authorization to deploy.

## Immediate acceptance package

The first real cycle should deliver a registered family, original source references, missing/excluded-case ledger, independent reports, a justified decision and one bottleneck retrospective. It need not produce a prompt change or blueprint. Use that package to choose the next engineering change; preserve the broader model stack and autonomous loop as goals rather than front-loading their construction.
