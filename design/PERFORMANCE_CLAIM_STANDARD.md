# Performance claim evidence standard

Use this standard for our results and external claims. It applies before words such as profitable, autonomous, validated, pays for itself or works.

## Evidence levels

- **Anecdote:** self-reported number or screenshot without reconciled records.
- **Traceable demonstration:** immutable logs and account/order exports for one declared run, including costs and failures; useful for operational proof, not alpha.
- **Research evidence:** frozen hypothesis, point-in-time data, realistic execution, development results, controls and full trial ledger.
- **Reserved validation:** untouched/prospective rules and outcomes, predefined statistics, concentration/risk/cost gates and complete missing-case accounting.
- **Execution evidence:** required live-quote/local-shadow sessions and hypothetical trades with fill-capacity audit.
- **Implementation consideration:** all gates pass and a separate human decision authorizes engineering. This is still not live trading authorization.

## Required reconciliation

Opening equity + deposits − withdrawals + realized P&L + unrealized executable liquidation value − fees/data/model/compute costs = ending economic equity. Explain timing and cash settlement. Preserve orders, fills, cancels, positions, corporate actions, unsupported assets and missing quotes. Do not value an illiquid position at its last trade when the claimed size could not exit near that price.

Report return, dollar P&L, maximum drawdown, gross exposure, turnover, leverage, largest loss, top-winner concentration, time at risk and benchmark under the same interval. Report all runs and accounts governed by the experiment, including zero-trade and failed runs. A selected winner is a case study, not an estimate of expectation.

## Agent-system attribution

To claim the network added value, compare predeclared alternatives: deterministic pipeline, single capable model and tiered network where affordable. Measure false approvals/rejections, critical evidence misses, rework, latency, failure recovery and full cost. Agent names or counts do not establish independent analysis; shared models/prompts/data can create correlated error.

No agent can certify its own performance. Ledger and validation review use independent code/artifacts. Marketing language is excluded from scientific summaries unless directly supported by the corresponding evidence level.
